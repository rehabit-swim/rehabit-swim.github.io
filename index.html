Rehabilitation as an ocean journey

Patients on long bed rest must repeat prescribed exercises every day, yet the routine is monotonous and most exergames assume a player who can stand or sit. Rehabit:Swim is built for the bed: each exercise becomes a swimming stroke that carries you toward an island.

Accessible
A low-cost motion sensor and a bedside touchscreen let patients play on their own, in bed.
Personalized
Clinicians prescribe the exercise, reps and sets; calibration sets each patient’s own thresholds.
Built for long stays
A bright, open sea replaces the hospital walls, and every finished session ends on an island.

Your movement moves the swimmer

Each exercise maps to a swimming stroke. Every valid repetition carries the swimmer closer to the island.

  • Shoulder PressBreaststroke

Perfect, Good, Bad

Fuller movements earn bigger ripples and bubbles, and even a small one gets a response.

One cycle, two roles

  1. PrescribeClinician
  2. PlayPatient
  3. RecordSystem
  4. AdjustClinician

Clinicians prescribe and adjust, patients play, and every session is logged for the next prescription.
